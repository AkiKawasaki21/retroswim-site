FAQ

  • Multiplayer can't find the other phones

    Bluetooth (up to 4 phones, no Wi-Fi needed): everyone opens MULTIPLAYER → BLUETOOTH, one phone hosts and the others join. Sit within a few rows of each other. It works in Airplane Mode as long as Bluetooth is turned back on. When iOS asks to use Bluetooth, tap Allow. If you declined, turn it back on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth.

    Wi-Fi (up to 8 phones): every phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network. No Wi-Fi around? Turn on Personal Hotspot on one phone and join it from the others. When iOS asks for Local Network access, tap Allow. If you declined, turn it back on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

  • How do I restore my purchases?

    Open the STORE in the game and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

  • Where is my save?

    Your career is saved on your device, not on a server, and there is no account. Deleting the app also deletes your save, so keep the app installed if you want to keep your career.

  • Do I need an internet connection?

    No. Flip Turn plays offline. Multiplayer uses Bluetooth or the local Wi-Fi network or hotspot, never the internet.
