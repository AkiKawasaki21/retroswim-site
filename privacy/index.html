Flip Turn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Flip Turn (formerly Retro Swim) is a game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with information.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking software.
  • Your career, settings and progress are saved only on your device.
  • The developer does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

What stays on your device

  • Save data: your career, swimmers, settings and purchase history are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Names you type: coach, swimmer and school names you enter are part of your save and are not sent to the developer.

Local multiplayer

Multiplayer races connect phones directly, either over the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot, or over Bluetooth. The nickname you choose and your race inputs and results are sent to the other phones in that room so the race can be shown. When you host a race over Bluetooth, the room name (your nickname) is broadcast to phones nearby so they can find it, and only phones that choose to join can race. Nothing from a multiplayer race is sent to the developer or stored on a server. iOS asks for Local Network permission for Wi-Fi races and for Bluetooth permission for Bluetooth races. You can decline either and still play everything else.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The game receives only confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock it. The developer never sees your payment details. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to the purchase itself.

School Cup

The School Cup is an optional leaderboard. In this version it runs in preview mode on your device and sends nothing.

If the online School Cup is turned on in a later version and you choose to join it, the game will send this to the game's server (hosted by Supabase): a random player ID created by the game, the school you picked, your role (for example swimmer or fan), the 1 to 3 initials you typed, an invite code if you entered one, and your weekly points. Other players see only initials and points. This data is used only to run the leaderboard. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to track you across apps. This policy will be updated when that happens.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove all data stored on your device.
  • To ask about or delete School Cup data once it is online, email the address below with your initials and school.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
