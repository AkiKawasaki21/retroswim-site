Heat sheet

Run the program. Swim the races.

  • Event 1

    Swim every race yourself

    • Nail the start, time your strokes, hold your underwaters and hit the wall.
    • All four strokes plus IM and relays, from the 50 free to the mile.
    • Manage energy and air. Go out too fast and you pay for it on the last lap.
    • Real swim rules: false starts and one-hand touches get you disqualified.
    • Photo finishes, personal bests and records.

  • Event 2

    Coach a college program

    • Men's and women's teams, with a full season: dual meets, a midseason invitational, the winter training trip, conference championships and nationals.
    • Set your lineup for every meet and decide who swims what.
    • Plan training and taper week by week. Push too hard and swimmers get tired or hurt.

  • Event 3

    Recruit and build

    • Scout and sign prospects from 1 to 5 stars, at home and abroad.
    • Work the transfer portal and balance a limited scholarship budget.
    • Upgrade your pool, weight room, sports medicine and staff.

  • Event 4

    Chase the dream

    • Walk on as yourself: create your own swimmer and see how far you go.
    • In the summer, race long course at Nationals, the Trials and the World Championships.
    • Every fourth year: the Summer Games. Make the team, reach the final, stand on the podium.

  • Event 5

    Race your friends

    • Local multiplayer for up to 8 phones on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. No account needed.
    • Quick Race: pick any event and field level and swim right now.

  • Event 6

    Made for phones

    • Plays offline
    • No ads
    • No account

    Short sessions: a race takes about a minute.

    Optional in-app purchases: Retro Swim PRO and Coaching Credit packs. Everything in the game can be earned by playing.
